## Primer: proof techniques

1. **Direct proof.** Start from assumptions and derive conclusion.
   - Example: if $n=2k$, then $n^2=4k^2=2(2k^2)$ is even.
2. **Contrapositive.** Prove $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ instead of $P\Rightarrow Q$.
   - Example: to show $n^2$ even $\Rightarrow n$ even, prove $n$ odd $\Rightarrow n^2$ odd.
3. **Contradiction.** Assume opposite and derive impossibility.
   - Example: assume $\sqrt{2}=p/q$ in lowest terms; parity contradiction follows.
4. **Induction.** Prove base case and inductive step.
   - Example: $1+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$.
