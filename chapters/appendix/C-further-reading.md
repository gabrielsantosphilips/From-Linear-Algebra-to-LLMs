# Appendix C — Further Reading

[← Table of Contents](../../README.md)

## Foundational papers

- **Vaswani et al. (2017), “Attention Is All You Need.”** The paper that
  introduced the transformer. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762>
- **Devlin et al. (2018), “BERT.”** Bidirectional encoder pre-training.
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805>
- **Radford et al. (2019), “Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask
  Learners” (GPT-2).** <https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf>
- **Brown et al. (2020), “Language Models are Few-Shot Learners” (GPT-3).**
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165>
- **Kaplan et al. (2020), “Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models.”**
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361>
- **Hoffmann et al. (2022), “Training Compute-Optimal LLMs” (Chinchilla).**
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556>
- **Hu et al. (2021), “LoRA.”** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685>

## Textbooks

- **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville, *Deep Learning* (MIT Press, 2016).** Free
  online: <https://www.deeplearningbook.org/>
- **Strang, *Introduction to Linear Algebra*.** The classic linear algebra text.
- **Bishop, *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*.** Probability & ML
  foundations.

## Visual & intuitive explainers

- **3Blue1Brown — “Essence of Linear Algebra”** (video series). The best
  geometric intuition for vectors, matrices, and eigenvectors.
  <https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra>
- **3Blue1Brown — Neural Networks / Transformers** series.
- **Jay Alammar, “The Illustrated Transformer.”**
  <https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/>
- **Andrej Karpathy, “Let's build GPT” / nanoGPT** — build a transformer from
  scratch in code. <https://github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT>
